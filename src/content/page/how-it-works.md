title: "How it works"
---

Most professions on this page are licensed by the **New York State Education Department (NYSED)**. The process is:

1. Download and complete **Form 1** for your profession.
2. Get Form 1 **notarized** (New York State requires this).
3. Submit your notarized Form 1 to TEF using the fee request form on your profession card.
4. TEF mails the form to New York State along with the licensure fee payment.

The **CASAC** credential is different — it's regulated by **OASAS**, not NYSED. For CASAC, you fill out the intake form first and TEF emails you a confirmation letter to upload with your OASAS application.

Allow the state 3–4 weeks to process your application. Only the applicant can receive status updates from NYSED, so direct any licensure status questions to NYSED at **518-474-3817** or via the [NYSED Contact Us form](https://www.op.nysed.gov/contact-us).
