**How long does the state take to process my application?**
Allow a few weeks. Only the applicant can receive status updates from NYSED — direct status questions to NYSED at 518-474-3817 or via their Contact Us form.

**Do I have to get Form 1 notarized?**
Yes, New York State requires Form 1 to be notarized before TEF can submit it.

**Can I submit the same request more than once?**
Please don't — duplicate submissions slow processing. Submit once and allow up to three weeks before following up.

**What if I'm applying for CASAC?**
CASAC uses a different process through OASAS. Submit the intake form on the CASAC card, then wait for TEF to email you a confirmation letter to upload with your OASAS application.

**What if my profession isn't listed here?**
Contact career services. This page only covers professions where TEF currently pays licensure or exam fees.
